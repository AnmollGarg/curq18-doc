# CRM

Track incoming leads, manage sales pipelines, assign opportunities to teams, and close deals in CURQ 18.

## Complete Visibility Over Your Sales Pipeline
With CRM, follow every commercial opportunity from first customer contact through negotiation and signed agreement. Organize deals visually using Kanban stages, adjust win probabilities, schedule follow-up activities, and collaborate with your team to convert leads into revenue.

## Structured Team Workflows and Performance Tracking
CRM allows sales leaders to allocate opportunities across specialized sales teams, establish monthly invoicing targets, and analyze why deals are won or lost. Connect opportunities directly with sales quotations and customer address records for a seamless commercial journey.
