OVERVIEW

# Chat

Coordinate team discussions, manage group channels, direct messages, voice calls, and record-level chatter across CURQ 18.

## Central Team Communication in Real Time
With Chat, connect with colleagues instantly across public channels, private project rooms, and direct one-on-one messages. Share documents, hold audio or video calls, and keep company-wide discussions organized without relying on external messaging tools.

## Seamless Collaboration Across Every Business Record
Chat powers the chatter feed on every customer card, sales quotation, project task, and invoice in CURQ. Log private internal notes, mention coworkers to ask questions, schedule follow-up activities, and send messages directly to clients from one unified communications center.
