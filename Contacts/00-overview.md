# Contacts

Manage companies, individual contacts, addresses, banking details, and localization settings in one address book.

## One Address Book for Customers, Vendors, and Partners
With Contacts, maintain a clean and connected directory for all your business relationships. Record company profiles, link employees and contact persons to parent organizations, and organize partners using custom contact tags and industry sectors.

## Complete Control Over Addresses, Banking, and Fiscal Information
Manage multiple delivery, invoice, and private addresses for every partner. Set up customer payment terms, default bank accounts, tax identifiers, and country-specific localization rules to ensure smooth operations across sales, purchasing, and accounting.
