OVERVIEW

# Website

Build, design, and manage enterprise web pages, multi-website architectures, and customer portal experiences in CURQ 18.

## Visual Drag-and-Drop Website Building
CURQ Website provides an intuitive visual editor for creating responsive, modern websites without writing code. Assemble pages using modular building blocks, customize color palettes and typography, optimize content for search engines, and deliver seamless mobile experiences.

## Multi-Website, SEO, and Portal Integration
Operate multiple independent websites from a single CURQ database with dedicated domain names, languages, and branding. Connect your website directly with CURQ apps including Sales, CRM, and customer portal for real-time customer interaction and lead capture.
