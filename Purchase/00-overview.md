OVERVIEW

# Purchase

Streamline vendor management, automate requests for quotation (RFQs), enforce purchase order approvals, track warehouse receipts, and manage vendor bills and bill control policies in CURQ 18.

## End-to-End Procurement Lifecycle
With Purchase, manage your entire supply chain and procurement lifecycle from a single operational workspace. Create and send Requests for Quotation (RFQs) to suppliers, compare offers, confirm purchase orders, and monitor vendor lead times. Keep operations aligned across purchasing, warehouse receiving, and accounts payable without data duplication.

## Purchasing Controls, Vendor Pricelists, and Bill Control
Maintain strict control over corporate expenditure using automated approval thresholds, blanket purchase agreements, and negotiated vendor pricelists with quantity-tiered discounts. Protect financial margins through automated bill control policies, ensuring vendor bills are validated only against verified received quantities and agreed purchase order terms.
