# Sales

Manage quotations and sales orders, configure pricing and products, and streamline fulfillment and invoicing.

## From Quotation to Order in One Place
With Sales, manage the complete commercial cycle from a single workspace. Build professional offers using Quotation Templates and the PDF Quote Builder, collect customer approvals with Online Signatures and Online Payments, and secure order processing with confirmed sales locking and pro-forma invoicing.

## Complete Control Over Pricing, Shipping, and Invoicing
Maintain full visibility over margins, pricelists, discounts, and product variants. Coordinate fulfillment using delivery methods and the Sendcloud shipping integration, and automate billing based on your preferred invoicing policy, whether invoicing ordered or delivered quantities.
